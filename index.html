Privacy Policy

In short: Based on the app's current functionality as described by its developer, Funora does not collect, transmit, or share users' personal data.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Funora mobile application for Android (package name: com.funora.android.app) handles information. It applies to the Funora app distributed through Google Play.

2. Information we collect

Funora does not collect personal information or sensitive user data. The app does not ask users to provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment details.

Funora does not transmit personal data from the app to the developer or to external servers.

3. How information is used

Because Funora does not collect or receive personal data, the developer does not use personal data for profiling, advertising, analytics, or other purposes.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Funora does not sell, rent, disclose, or share users' personal data with third parties. No personal data is transferred by the app to third-party recipients.

5. Storage, security, and retention

The app does not transmit personal data to the developer or store personal data on the developer's servers. Accordingly, the developer does not retain a collection of users' personal data and there is no server-side personal data to delete.

Any information that a user independently chooses to keep on their own device remains under the user's control and can be removed by uninstalling the app or using the device's app-storage controls, where applicable.

6. Children's privacy

Funora does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If you believe that the app is collecting information contrary to this policy, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy
